About HANGUK SKIN

What is HANGUK SKIN?

HANGUK SKIN (Hanguk Skin Private Limited) is an Indian company based in Gurugram, Haryana. We officially import and distribute genuine Korean beauty brands in India and support our retail partners with marketing.

Is HANGUK SKIN an Indian company?

Yes. Hanguk Skin Private Limited is registered in India, CIN U46491HR2026PTC147722, with its registered office at 703, 7th Floor, Palm Court, Sector-16, MG Road, Gurugram, Haryana 122007.

What does "Hanguk" mean?

Hanguk means Korea in Korean. It is the only place our brands come from.

Products and authenticity

Do you only carry Korean brands?

Yes. We carry Korean beauty brands only, and we plan to keep it that way as we grow.

Are your products genuine?

Yes. Every product we supply is imported officially by HANGUK SKIN, carries our importer details on the pack and comes with its documents. We do not deal in grey-market or parallel imports.

Are your products registered with CDSCO?

Products we import are registered for import with CDSCO, and the registration number (RC No.) is printed on the pack.

Can I see import documents?

Yes. Partners can request the import registration, invoices and batch details for products we supply.

What categories do you bring in?

Skincare such as serums, essences and moisturisers, plus sun care, cleansers, sheet masks and makeup.

Working with us

Who do you supply?

Beauty and skincare stores, retail chains and online marketplace sellers across India.

They depend on your range and channel. We share them in writing after our first conversation.

Do you help with marketing?

Yes. We support the brands we carry with online, offline and digital marketing: marketplace listings, in-store displays, sampling, staff training, and social and creator content.

For shoppers

How can I tell if a Korean product is genuine?

Check the back of the pack for the Indian importer name and address, the RC No., an MRP in rupees, the country of origin, batch and dates, and consumer care details. See the full guide.

Where can I buy your products?

We supply retail stores and online marketplaces in India. Look for Hanguk Skin Private Limited as the importer on the pack.
