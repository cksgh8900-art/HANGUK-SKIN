Our story

Indian-led. Built on trust.

HANGUK SKIN was started by a family with roots in both Korea and India. We knew what Korean skincare could do, and we kept seeing how little of what reached India was the real thing.

So we built a company in Gurugram with one rule: only genuine Korean products, brought in officially, with our name on every pack.

Where we're going From Korea's best to India's everyday.

Today we import, distribute and market Korean beauty. As we grow, we'll keep finding new ways to bring it closer to Indian consumers.
